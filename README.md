### 🚀 About Me

I'm a GenAI engineer based in Bengaluru with 3+ years of experience building LLM applications, RAG pipelines and multi-agent systems. I also work on Responsible AI, including evaluation, hallucination mitigation and bias detection. My projects range from a multilingual legal-rights assistant for women in India to governed enterprise agents with policy checks and human escalation. I'm open to new roles.

🔭 &nbsp;I'm currently working on **governed multi-agent systems with evaluation and human escalation**  
🌱 &nbsp;I'm currently learning **n8n agent workflows and LLM evaluation**  
👯 &nbsp;I'm looking to collaborate on **open-source RAG and agent tools, or AI for underserved communities**  
🤔 &nbsp;I'm looking for help with **deploying multi-agent apps beyond free-tier hosting**  
💬 &nbsp;Ask me about **RAG pipelines, multi-agent systems (LangGraph, CrewAI), hallucination mitigation and LLM evaluation, voice AI in Indian languages**
