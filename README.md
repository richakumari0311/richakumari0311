### 📂 Featured Projects

**[HerRight](https://github.com/richakumari0311/HerRight)**  
Voice-enabled assistant that explains legal rights to women in India, in 7 Indian languages, grounded in official government documents.  
`LangChain` `FAISS` `Gemini 2.5` `Sarvam AI` `Streamlit`

**[Enterprise Copilot](https://github.com/richakumari0311/enterprise-copilot)**  
Governed assistant that routes HR, Finance and Support questions to LangGraph agents, grounds answers in policy documents with RAG, checks each answer against policy rules, and sends low-confidence cases to a human. Includes audit logging and an evaluation harness with measured results.  
`LangGraph` `ChromaDB` `FastAPI` `Postgres` `Streamlit`

**[Food Delivery Strategy Agents](https://github.com/richakumari0311/Food-Delivery-Strategy-Agents)**  
Five LangGraph agents that analyze Swiggy and Zomato by combining order data, Play Store reviews (RAG) and live competitor research into evidence-cited strategy recommendations. Four agents run in parallel and a strategy agent merges their output. The order data is synthetic; the review and competitor inputs are real.  
`LangGraph` `Gemini` `pgvector` `Tavily`

**[AI Research Agent](https://github.com/richakumari0311/AI-Research-Agent)**  
Multi-agent system that researches BFSI topics and writes structured reports, combining web search with RAG.  
`CrewAI` `ChromaDB` `Groq (LLaMA 3.3 70B)` `Serper`

**[MarketMind](https://github.com/richakumari0311/MarketMind)** ([live demo](https://marketmind-090i.onrender.com))  
Marketing campaign generator with research, strategy, content and critique agents. Streams progress live over SSE and keeps memory across runs.  
`Google ADK` `FastAPI` `Ollama / Anthropic` `Serper`

**Smaller projects:** [RAG Knowledge Assistant](https://github.com/richakumari0311/rag-knowledge-assistant) (Q&A over your own documents, with sources) and [AI Support Bot](https://github.com/richakumari0311/ai-support-bot) (customer support bot that runs fully offline)
